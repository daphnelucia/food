# pancakes
<img src="assets/pancakes/Pasted%20image%2020261121192012.png" width=230>
for 10 pancakes, 2 hours (1 hour making the mix, 1 hour cooking)

## ingredients
* 4 eggs
* sugar
* vanilla extract
* butter
* milk
* honey
* flour
* baking powder

## preparation
into a big bowl pour then mix:
<ul>
<li>4 eggs</li>
<img width=224 src="assets/pancakes/Pasted%20image%2020261121191413.png">
<li>~80g sugar</li>
<li>1-2 tbsp vanilla extract</li>
<img width=234 src="assets/pancakes/Pasted%20image%2020261121191440.png">
<li>in another plate, melt about this much of butter in the microwave for 50 seconds. then pour into the mix</li>
<img width=261 src="assets/pancakes/Pasted%20image%2020261121191500.png">
<img width=269 src="assets/pancakes/Pasted%20image%2020261121191511.png">
<li>200ml milk</li>
<img width=262 src="assets/pancakes/Pasted%20image%2020261121191524.png">
<li>pour honey for like 10 seconds</li>
<li>~250g flour, mix softly until no unmixed flour is visible (some lumps are okay)</li>
<img width=305 src="assets/pancakes/Pasted%20image%2020261121191557.png">
<li>1-2tbsp baking powder (about 2/3 of 15g packet), keep mixing until this consistency</li>
<img width=302 src="assets/pancakes/batter.avif">
</ul>

mix is ready to cook in medium-high heat. butter the pan every 1-2 pancakes if you want. cook each side for 2-4 minutes depending on size<br>
