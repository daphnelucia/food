# pancakes
![230](assets/pancakes/Pasted%20image%2020261121192012.png)
for 10 pancakes, 2 hours (1 hour making the mix, 1 hour cooking)

## ingredients
* 4 eggs
* sugar
* vanilla extract
* butter
* milk
* honey
* flour
* baking powder

## preparation
into a big bowl pour then mix:
* 4 eggs
![224](assets/pancakes/Pasted%20image%2020261121191413.png)
* ~80g sugar
* 1-2 tbsp vanilla extract
![234](assets/pancakes/Pasted%20image%2020261121191440.png)
* in another plate, melt about this much of butter in the microwave for 50 seconds. then pour into the mix
![261](assets/pancakes/Pasted%20image%2020261121191500.png)
![269](assets/pancakes/Pasted%20image%2020261121191511.png)
* 200ml milk
![262](assets/pancakes/Pasted%20image%2020261121191524.png)
* pour honey for like 10 seconds
* ~250g flour, mix softly until no unmixed flour is visible (some lumps are okay)
![305](assets/pancakes/Pasted%20image%2020261121191557.png)
* 1-2tbsp baking powder (about 2/3 of 15g packet), keep mixing until this consistency
![302](assets/pancakes/batter.avif)

mix is ready to cook in medium-high heat. butter the pan every 1-2 pancakes if you want. cook each side for 2-4 minutes depending on size
![306](assets/pancakes/Pasted%20image%2020261121192518.png)
